# Choosing a country shows the local stablecoin and a live quote

## Open Keel Jobs
Open the page and switch the language to EN with the EN button at the top.

## Pick Brazil
Choose "Brazil · wBRL" in the Country select.
Verify the text "You'll be paid in wBRL." appears under the select.
Verify task B shows a quote in the form "$0.10 ≈ <number> wBRL" with a number between 0.3 and 1.2.

## Pick Nigeria
Choose "Nigeria · cNGN". Verify the note now says cNGN and the quote shows a cNGN amount above 50.
