# Refuse a payee who is not on the allowlist

## Open the desk
Open the page and wait until the instruction box is visible.

## Try to pay a stranger
Type "pay Sofia (Brazil) $0.10 for testing" into the instruction box and send it. Wait for Keel's reply.

## Nothing was sent
Verify the reply says Sofia is not on the allowlist and that nothing was sent.
In the "Receipts" panel, verify there is no receipt for Sofia.
